# cosmo-correlators
Lecture notes on "Cosmological Correlations" written together with Austin Joyce (Chicago).
The lectures were delivered at BUSSTEP 2022 (Imperial College, London), the 
Kavli Asian Winter School on Strings, Particles and Cosmology 2023 (Korea), the Advanced Summer School in Quantum Field Theory and Quantum Gravity 2023 (Vietnam), the Cargese Summer School 2025, TASI 2026 and BUSSTEP 2026 (Cambridge).
